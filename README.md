### Core Expertise

- **Cross-Platform Engineering** — Production Flutter apps on Android & iOS, pixel-perfect across phones, tablets, and foldables.
- **State Management & Architecture** — `Bloc`, `Provider`, `GetX`. Feature-first folders, SOLID, DI, testable boundaries.
- **Networking** — Battle-tested `Dio` / `http` layers with interceptors, token refresh, retry/backoff, and offline-first caching.
- **Performance** — DevTools profiling, killing rebuild storms, GPU/CPU tuning for smooth 60–120 FPS pipelines.
- **Native Bridges** — Platform Channels into Kotlin & Swift; **Rust FFI** for compute-heavy modules at native speed.
- **OTA Releases** — Shorebird code-push to ship Dart patches in minutes — no store wait.
- **Monetization** — Play Store In-App Billing, subscriptions, plus **SSLCommerz** & **bKash** integrations for the BD market.
- **Adaptive UI** — Responsive layouts that degrade gracefully across device classes and platform idioms.
